Strudel DJ

A 129M-parameter model (strudel-dj-129m) writes a short Strudel program: drums, bass, chords and lead in one stack(...). The model runs in your browser, and the player below is strudel.cc itself.

The first run downloads about 130 MB of weights (int8), once; later visits load them from your browser's cache. A program then takes roughly 5–30 s, depending on your computer. Code: github.com/nenkoru/strudel-dj (AGPL-3.0) · work report · weights CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Built mostly by AI (Claude, Anthropic) under the author's direction.

Open in strudel.cc In the player, press ▶ (or Ctrl+Enter); the first notes load samples for a few seconds.

Known weak spots: the bass is often one note repeated, and the model sometimes writes past the end of a pattern. If the brackets don't balance or it sounds off, try another seed. The model picks its own tempo; the page replaces it with the slider's.
